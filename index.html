BACKGROUND & OBJECTIVES

About the Winter School

Academic Scope & Vision

"From Information to Knowledge: Information Retrieval and Natural Language Processing" connects Information Retrieval with Natural Language Processing, Information Extraction, Knowledge Representation, Semantic Search, Question Answering, and Multilingual Information Access through conceptual lectures, hands-on laboratories, and research-oriented discussions.

Participants will engage with foundational concepts as well as cutting-edge research paradigms under the direct guidance of prominent academics and industry researchers.

Conceptual & algorithmic lectures
Hands-on Python laboratory modules
Research problem identification
1-on-1 interaction with leading faculty

ACADEMIC SYLLABUS

List of Topics Covered

TOPIC 01

Foundations of Information Retrieval

Inverted index, Boolean models, tokenization, stemming, lemmatization, and text preprocessing baselines.

TOPIC 02

Retrieval Models, Ranking and Evaluation

Vector space models, TF-IDF, BM25, Learning to Rank, MAP, NDCG, trec_eval, and ir_measures metrics.

TOPIC 03

Foundations of Natural Language Processing

Language modeling, POS tagging, dependency parsing, sequence labeling, and classical statistical NLP.

TOPIC 04

Language Representation & Semantic Processing

Word embeddings, contextual representation, self-attention, and Hugging Face transformer architectures.

TOPIC 05

Information Extraction & Knowledge Discovery

Named Entity Recognition (NER), relation extraction, Knowledge Graphs, and entity linking.

TOPIC 06

Semantic Search & Question Answering

Dense passage retrieval, vector databases (FAISS), RAG models, and open-domain QA.

TOPIC 07

Multilingual & Indian-Language Information Access

Cross-lingual IR, Indic-NLP encoders, transliteration, and low-resource text processing.

TOPIC 08

From Information to Knowledge: Research Directions

Research problem identification, open challenges, hallucination, and future research pathways.

Hands-on Code

Practical modules on text retrieval, information extraction, semantic search, and question answering in Python.

Background & Recommended Prerequisites

  • Basic knowledge of Python programming, data structures, and algorithms.
  • Discrete mathematics and introductory machine learning concepts.
  • Prior exposure to Natural Language Processing or IR is helpful, but not mandatory.

Software Stack & Environments

Python Jupyter / Google Colab NLTK spaCy Hugging Face Transformers Elasticsearch / Lucene FAISS trec_eval ir_measures
